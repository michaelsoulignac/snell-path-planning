A Drone That Follows Light

A drone flies through successive wind zones and has to reach a target point. It can be shown mathematically that, in each wind zone, the drone is deflected like a light ray, following a law analogous to Snell's. To reach the target, it is therefore enough to adjust the initial heading of the drone, and nothing else. Try moving the ordinate of the target, then finding the optimal initial heading, or press Solve: the planner finds it for you, by bisection.
